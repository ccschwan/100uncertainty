Fairness asks whether a system treats different groups or individuals appropriately under a given set of criteria. This question often emerges once a model is deployed in a real-world setting.

## Fairness is not a single measure

There are many notions of fairness, and no single definition captures all concerns. Depending on the context, trade-offs may be necessary between:

- statistical parity,
- equal opportunity,
- calibration,
- transparency,
- social impact.

Fairness is therefore a technical and ethical problem at the same time.
