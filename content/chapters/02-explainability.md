Explainability is about making the logic of a method understandable to humans. In systems where predictions influence decisions, this is often as important as predictive performance.

## Why explanation matters

A high-performing model can still be hard to trust if it hides its reasoning. Explanations help us detect failure modes, challenge assumptions, and communicate results.

## Key themes

Interpretability is not just a technical issue. It is also a communication and governance issue. In practice, it matters for:

- debugging models,
- auditing decisions,
- understanding feature effects,
- communicating uncertainty.
